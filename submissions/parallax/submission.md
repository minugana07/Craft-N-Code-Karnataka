## Project summary

Parallax is an AI-powered teacher intelligence platform that helps educators connect assessment evidence to learning gaps, plan targeted interventions, and track follow-up outcomes. It supports teacher decision-making rather than replacing teachers, helping educators look beyond scores to understand where students may need support.

## Short answers

### 1. What made you choose this problem?

Student performance is often reduced to marks, which may not show the specific concepts a student is struggling with or whether support has helped. Teachers need a clearer way to connect assessment evidence with learning gaps and follow-up actions. We chose this problem to help educators make more informed, evidence-based decisions while keeping teachers in control.

### 2. Who is it for, and how would they use it in a normal week?

Parallax is designed for college educators who need to monitor student learning across classes and assessments. During a normal week, a teacher could review class-level performance, identify possible learning gaps, record targeted interventions, and revisit follow-up results. This creates a continuous workflow from assessment to action rather than treating each test as an isolated event.

### 3. If you had another month, what would you build next?

We would strengthen the AI-assisted analysis and validate its usefulness with real educators and appropriately protected student data. We would add clearer evidence behind suggested learning gaps, improve intervention tracking, and evaluate whether follow-up insights help teachers plan their next steps. We would also improve deployment, testing, and documentation for reliable use beyond the prototype.
